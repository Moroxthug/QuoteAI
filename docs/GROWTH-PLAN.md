# Growth, pricing and owner analytics plan (Phases 131+)

Decided by the owner on 2026-10-19. Launch market: **Canada only**. Prices in **CAD**. Never build from superseded plans.

## What the owner asked for
1. A hook: new users get **3 free quotes**, then pay.
2. Launch price **$19/month**, rising to **$29/month automatically at 100 paying customers**. People who subscribe at $19 keep it (founding price, for life).
3. Plan for **Google Play Billing** (Canada is not in Google's billing-choice markets, so in-app purchase on Android must use Play Billing; the owner lifted the "no prices in the app" rule for Android on 2026-10-19). Stripe stays for the web.
4. Automated sign-up funnels and AI outreach channels, built and operated by Claude, with the owner approving what goes out.
5. **One place that shows everything the owner needs to know**, with financial control (revenue, costs, profit, tax, cash).

## What already exists (do not rebuild)
- `GET /api/admin/metrics` (users, MRR by list price, quotes), `/admin/margin` (AI cost per user vs plan price), `/admin/quote-stats`, `/admin/widget/stats`, `/admin/search-console`, `/admin/email-events`, `/admin/ops`, `/admin/automations`; the admin page is `artifacts/quote-ai/src/pages/admin.tsx`.
- Cost capture: `usage_events` / `usage_daily_summary` (AI tokens, WhatsApp, SMS, email, storage; `lib/usage.ts`).
- Plans: `lib/db/src/schema/plans.ts` (ids free, monthly_starter, monthly_pro, monthly_business, monthly_elite), mirrored in `artifacts/quote-ai/src/lib/plans.ts`. Price list in `routes/payments.ts` (`PLANS`).
- A web trial already exists: 7 days and 3 PDF downloads (`getTrialStatus` in `routes/payments.ts`). The Pocket app enforces nothing yet.
- Stripe webhook in `app.ts` (checkout completed, subscription created/updated/deleted) writes the plan on the profile. It stores **no payment history**.
- Not captured anywhere: where a user came from (source), funnel events before signup, real payments, refunds, costs that are not AI usage, marketing spend, referrals.

## Rules for this work
- Money is stored in **integer cents**, with the currency. Dates in UTC, reported in America/Toronto.
- Every number on the HQ page says where it comes from and the period; nothing is invented. When a source is missing (for example Play fees before Play Billing exists) the page says "not connected", never a zero that looks real.
- Owner-only: every HQ route sits behind `requireAdmin`; no HQ data reaches customers.
- Outreach follows CASL: express consent for sign-ups; cold B2B email only on the conspicuous-publication basis with a record of source, date and relevance, sender identity and a working unsubscribe; no cold texts; an approval queue before anything is sent.
- New env vars are read by name and added to `scripts/src/env-inventory.ts`; routes need the route-matrix entry; migrations are plain SQL in `lib/db/drizzle`, applied with `supabase db query --linked`.

### As built (Phase 132 backend, `routes/admin-hq.ts` + `src/hq/`)
Everything above holds. Where the line above says only "→ something", this is the exact shape. Differences from the lines above are marked **(changed)** or **(added)**.
- Errors: `400 { error }` for a bad query or body (`issues: [{path,message}]` on bodies), `404` for a missing id, `403` for non-admins. Range: `from`/`to` inclusive Toronto dates, default last 30 days, max 800 days.
- `GET /overview`: as in the contract plus **(added)** `money.contractionMrrCents` (downgrades that stay subscribed). **(changed)** `unit.grossMarginPct` is `number | null` (null when there is no revenue). `unit.arpuCents` is 0 with nobody paying; `ltvCents` and `paybackMonths` are null when not computable (LTV needs 10+ recorded paying customers at the start of a 28+ day range, and some churn). `ops.cronOk` / `ops.deadRuns` are null if ops health cannot be read; `ops.emailBounceRate` is a fraction (0.0123) or null with no email events. `funnel[].stage` is one of `visits, signups, first_quote, quote_sent, quote_accepted, paying`; `rateFromPrevious` is a fraction (0.4 = 40%) or null (first stage, or the previous stage is 0). Funnel stages after `signups` count only companies that signed up inside the range; `paying` is a share of `signups` (not of the previous stage); every rate is within 0..1 and null when its base is 0. Test/QA accounts are excluded from every HQ number (see `notes`). `sources[].cacCents` is null when no spend is recorded. `sources[].source` includes `unknown` (no attribution recorded). `costsCents` has exactly the six keys (no total); usage costs are metered in US cents and not converted. `notes` lists every estimate, missing source and exclusion: show them.
- `GET /series?metric=&bucket=&from=&to=` → `[{ at: 'YYYY-MM-DD' (bucket start, Toronto; weeks start Monday), value }]`, every bucket in the range present (zeros filled). `metric=mrr` is MRR at the end of each bucket (cents); `signups`/`quotes` are counts; `revenue`/`costs`/`profit` are cents. `bucket` defaults to `day`. Revenue and profit include the list-price estimate for subscribers without billing events (no flag per point: use `/overview` notes).
- **(changed)** `GET /cohorts?months=12` → `{ months, cohorts: [{ month: 'YYYY-MM', signups, active: (number|null)[], paying: (number|null)[] }], notes }`. Index k = k months after signup; values are percent of that month's signups (0-100, one decimal); null = not reached yet, or no billing history that far back. `active` = created a quote that month; `paying` = had a recorded paid subscription at month end.
- **(changed)** `GET /customers?sort=since|company|plan|revenue|aiCost|margin&dir=asc|desc&q=&limit=100&offset=0` → `{ customers: [{ userId, companyName, email, plan, status, interval, since, source, founding, mtdRevenueCents, estimated, aiCostCents, costCents, marginCents, lastActiveAt }], total, limit, offset, month, notes }`. Revenue is this month so far, ex tax; `estimated: true` means the plan list price is shown because the company has no billing events yet. Costs are in US cents.
- `GET /expenses?from&to` → `{ range, items: [{ id, name, category, amountCents, currency, cadence, startsOn, endsOn, note, createdAt, countedCentsInRange }], totalInRangeCents }`. `POST /expenses` body `{ name, category?, amountCents (int > 0), cadence: one_off|monthly|yearly, startsOn, endsOn?, note? }` → `201` the row. `DELETE /expenses/:id` → `{ ok: true }`. Expenses in category `tax` are listed but left out of costs and profit.
- `GET /marketing-spend?from&to` → `{ range, items: [{ id, channel, campaign, spentOn, amountCents, currency, note, createdAt }], totalCents, byChannel: [{ channel, amountCents }], truncated }`. `POST` body `{ channel, campaign?, spentOn, amountCents, note? }` (channel is lower-cased; use the same names as `sources[].source`) → `201` the row. `DELETE /marketing-spend/:id` → `{ ok: true }`.
- **(added)** `GET /settings/cash` → `{ cashCents: number|null, updatedAt: string|null }`; `PUT /settings/cash` body `{ cashCents: int >= 0 | null }` (null clears it). Runway = cash / the period's average monthly net burn, null when cash is unknown or the period did not burn cash.
- `GET /export.csv?kind=revenue|expenses|customers&from&to` → UTF-8 CSV download (`Content-Disposition: attachment`). Revenue rows are Stripe payments, refunds and disputes (refunds/disputes negative) with gross, tax and ex-tax columns; expenses also lists marketing spend; customers is a snapshot of this month. Estimated revenue is never in the revenue CSV.
